The host

DBATU, Lonere

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere, is proud to host Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 in Konkan for the first time. This edition brings together students, faculty members, and research scholars to explore the exciting world of quantum computing through a hands-on workshop followed by a hackathon, inspired by the Fall Fest format followed by universities around the world.

How the fest unfolds

Three days, building on each other

Day 1

Qiskit Basics

Start from first principles — quantum computing fundamentals and algorithm complexity — then get hands-on with two Qiskit lab sessions. No quantum background needed, just curiosity and a laptop.

  • Introduction to Quantum Computing
  • Introduction to Qiskit
  • Algorithm Analysis & Complexity
  • Two hands-on Qiskit Lab sessions
Day 2

Quantum Algorithms with Qiskit

Go deeper into the algorithms that make quantum computing useful — optimization and error correction — with two more lab sessions to put the theory into practice.

  • Quantum Algorithms
  • Quantum Optimization Algorithms
  • Error Correction with Qiskit
  • Qiskit Lab 2 & Lab 3
Day 3

Applying Qiskit to Industrial Problems

Apply everything to real optimization problems, then build and defend your solution in front of a judging panel before the closing farewell.

  • Portfolio Optimization with Qiskit
  • Route Optimization with Qiskit
  • Hackathon problem statement & build time
  • Presentations & farewell

Good to know

FAQ

Do I need a quantum computing background?

No. Day 1 workshops start from first principles — qubits, gates, and measurement — before anyone touches the hackathon problem statements.

What team size is allowed for the hackathon?

Teams of 2–4 for the General Track. The Freshers Track is individual.

Do I need my own quantum hardware?

No. Everything runs through Qiskit on simulators, with access to real IBM Quantum backends provided during the event.

Is there a registration fee?

No, participation is free for all DBATU students.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — every participant who submits a project receives a certificate, with additional recognition for winners.
